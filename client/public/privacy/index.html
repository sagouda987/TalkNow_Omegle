Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026

This page explains how information is used when you visit TalkNow or join a live conversation.

Live conversations

Text, audio and video that you send are shared with the participant you connect to. Camera and microphone access require your browser permission. Browser-to-browser connections use WebRTC and a signalling service to establish the connection; connection information may include network addresses and session identifiers. Other participants may capture what you share, so avoid sensitive or identifying information.

Community chat

Messages submitted to the live community chat are sent to the chat service, shared with connected visitors and included in recent chat history. Do not post private information in this public feed.

Moderation and browser storage

The site uses browser local storage for moderation records, including reports and penalties. A report may include the reported message, session identifiers, reason and time. Reports can also be sent to the signalling service. Clearing site data removes local records from your browser.

Analytics and hosting

TalkNow loads Google Analytics to measure visits and usage events. Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers. The website is hosted on Vercel and uses a separate service for chat signalling. These services receive information needed to handle requests, such as network and device information.

Your controls

You can decline or revoke camera and microphone permissions in your browser, disconnect from a conversation, clear browser site data, and use browser privacy controls. Disconnecting does not erase information another participant has already received.

Age and contact

TalkNow is intended for adults aged 18 and over. For privacy questions or requests, email talknow047@gmail.com.
